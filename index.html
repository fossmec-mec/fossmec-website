# About FOSSMEC

FOSSMEC is the Free and Open Source Software Cell of Government Model Engineering College, Thrikkakara. It brings together students passionate about technology to learn, collaborate and contribute to the open source community.

## Objectives

  • Promote hands-on learning and exploration of open source technologies.
  • Organise workshops, technical sessions and community events.
  • Encourage students to collaborate on projects and contribute to open source.

## Community

FOSSMEC is open to all students interested in learning, experimenting and building together.
